Summary

Hey! My name is Yuki Teru, but you can simply call me Yuki.

I’m a cosplayer, performer, and creative who enjoys bringing characters and ideas to life through different forms of entertainment: cosplay appearances, interactive events, dance performances, and I also offer crochet workshops such as crochet and creative makeup.

I’m always open to trying new things, whether it’s hosting, participating in unique activities, or taking on a new event concept. If it’s something within my abilities, I’d be more than happy to explore it and create something memorable with you!
